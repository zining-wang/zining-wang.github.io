Welcome to my website! I am an Assistant Professor of Marketing at Carroll School of Management, Boston College.
I am an empirical modeler studying **digital platforms, prosocial behavior, and the creator economy**. My work combines causal inference with machine learning and large language models.
I hold a Ph.D. from the University of British Columbia, an M.Sc. from Erasmus University, and a BEcon. from Beijing Foreign Studies University.

You can find my CV [here](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/u9ni2skvxcahaj4tg0s22/CV_Zining_Wang_20260928.pdf?rlkey=ckdtwzlw7ztgz3af4ac4qv8mu&st=kkrcdytx&dl=0).
